The four systems

Keep each finding connected to its evidence.

The app brings the property record, guided inspection, labeled photos and form preparation into the same job. You verify the source data and decide what belongs in the report.

Roof

Organize covering, condition, age and update information with roof photos. Where public permit records are available, use the source link to check the supporting history; a missing record is not proof of a roof’s age.

Electrical

Keep panel information, observed wiring, condition and supporting documentation together. Review the entries and unresolved issues before preparing the form.

HVAC

Record the equipment and observed condition, with readable equipment and manufacturer-plate photos. Check any extracted information against the equipment you inspected.

Plumbing

Organize water-heater and visible plumbing findings with the relevant photographs. Keep supply, drain and update details supported by what you observed and verified.

Before you finish the site visit

A 4-Point photo-preparation checklist.

Use the form required for your assignment as the final checklist. The Citizens sample form currently linked from its March 2025 bulletin is labeled Insp4pt 03 25.

  • Exterior and roof: views of every dwelling side and each roof slope.
  • Plumbing: the water heater including its temperature-pressure relief valve, under-cabinet plumbing and drains, and exposed valves.
  • Electrical: the main service panel and interior door label, plus the panel-off view specified in the form.
  • HVAC: equipment photographs that include the dated manufacturer’s plate.
  • Observed issues: photographs documenting the hazards or deficiencies you report.

Source: Citizens 4-Point sample form, Insp4pt 03 25 (PDF). This is a preparation aid, not a substitute for the complete form or your professional assessment.

What changed in 2025? Citizens’ March 20 bulletin highlights the water-heater relief-valve photo, additional plumbing age/material details, electrical remediation documentation and clarified HVAC questions. Read the official update and check the current inspections resources for your assignment. Sources checked September 10, 2026.

Inside Inspector Gadgets

From a job address to your reviewed PDF.

  1. Create the job and confirm the property. Add the address and available property research. Verify details against their sources; permit access varies by jurisdiction.
  2. Work through the four systems. Add findings and label the supporting photos in the guided inspection. Resolve missing information while the evidence is available.
  3. Review the form entries. Check the property, systems, photos and inspector details. The product keeps unverified fields blank or marked for verification; your judgment controls the final report.
  4. Preview, then choose your export. A free account lets you preview the completed work as a watermarked SAMPLE. Unlock a clean report or choose a plan when you are ready.

Before your first 4-Point report.

Can I try the workflow before paying?

Yes. Create a free account without a card and preview your work as a watermarked SAMPLE. Clean exports are paid: $5 for a single report, $20/month for up to 10 inspections, or $98/year for unlimited inspections. See the current plans and renewal terms.

Is this where a homeowner books a 4-Point inspection?

No. Inspector Gadgets is software used by inspectors to organize and prepare reports. Homeowners should arrange the physical inspection with an appropriately qualified inspection professional.

Does a 4-Point replace a full home inspection or a wind mitigation report?

These are different workflows. The 4-Point focuses on the four named systems; Inspector Gadgets also supports a full-home report and the separate wind mitigation form. Confirm the assignment before choosing the report.
