What a Florida rental walkthrough should cover

  • Water everywhere it runs: check under every sink for staining or soft cabinet floors, run each faucet and flush each toilet, and look at the water heater’s age label and the pan under it.
  • A/C and airflow: run the system, feel for cold air at the vents, and look at the air handler for rust or standing water in the drain pan — in Florida a struggling A/C shows up on the first hot day, not at signing.
  • Ceilings and around windows: water stains, bubbling paint, or soft drywall usually mean a roof or window leak that predates you.
  • Electrical basics: test outlets in kitchen and baths (GFCI buttons should trip and reset), and note any warm switch plates or breakers that look scorched.
  • Doors, locks, and windows: every exterior door should lock, every window should open and latch — that’s security and your hurricane-season exit.
  • Signs of pests and mold: droppings under sinks and in closets, musty smell in bathrooms or near the A/C closet, dark speckling on vents.
  • Smoke detectors: press the test button in each bedroom and hallway.
  • Photograph everything on day one: date-stamped photos of every existing ding, stain, and scratch are what get a deposit back.
