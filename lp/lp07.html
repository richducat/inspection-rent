The workflow it was built around

What happens on a job, step by step

01

Type the address — the records are pre-filled

The county property record, owner information, year built, square footage, and flood zone are pulled for the address and dropped into the job file. No hunting through the appraiser’s site before you leave the driveway.

02

Permit history, pulled

The permit history — including the roof permit date that wind mitigation reports hinge on — comes in with the property data, instead of a separate trip through the county permit portal.

03

Walk the house with the checklist

Guided checklists and required photos keep the walkthrough in order, so any inspector on the crew captures the same evidence in the same places.

04

The forms are generated

The 4-Point, the OIR-B1-1802 wind mitigation, and a complete custom inspection report come out as finished PDFs, built from the records and the walkthrough — verify, sign, send.

That’s the whole case study. No invented user counts, no star ratings — one firm it was built with, one customer’s words, and the workflow itself.
