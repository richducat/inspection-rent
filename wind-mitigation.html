Rev. 04/26 — the form changed this year

Every wind mit on or after April 1, 2026 uses the new 1802.

Florida revised the Uniform Mitigation Verification Inspection form to OIR-B1-1802 Rev. 04/26 — with more documentation to support each answer, including performance-based roof-to-wall attachment options. The new form asks for more, and the kitchen-table problem didn't go anywhere. Inspector Gadgets prepares its wind mitigation output on the configured Rev. 04/26 template, so the paperwork keeps up with the rule change instead of you.

Roof age — with a paper trail

A permit number and an issue date ends the conversation.

The question that decides a wind mit is how old that roof really is, and it usually gets answered with a squint at the shingles and a homeowner's best guess. Where county records are available, Inspector Gadgets pulls the roof permit from the county system: permit number, issue date, source link. The roof age on your form traces to a public record a carrier can check.
