Rev. 04/26 — the form changed this year

The updated 1802 took effect April 1, 2026.

The Florida Office of Insurance Regulation lists the updated Uniform Mitigation Verification Inspection Form as effective April 1, 2026. Inspector Gadgets uses the configured Rev. 04/26 template. Check the official OIR resources and current six-page form (PDF) when preparing your assignment.

Roof age — with a paper trail

Start with the record. Verify what it proves.

Where public records are available, Inspector Gadgets brings permit information and its source link into the job. Those records are a research starting point. A permit issue date is not automatically the application date or the installation year requested by the current form.

  • Permit identifiers and available dates, with source links
  • Property records and permit records are distinct sources
  • Coverage across Florida counties, growing — see coverage
  • Verify roof-covering evidence against Question 4 of the current form

Question 4 asks for a permit application date or FBC/MDC product approval number, together with the original installation/replacement year, or an indication that verification information was unavailable. Confirm the relevant evidence; do not substitute an issue date for an application date.
